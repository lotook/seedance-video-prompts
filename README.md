# Seedance Video Prompt Recipes

Prompt recipes for short AI videos with Seedance models (1.5 Pro, 2.0 Mini, 2.0 Fast, 2.0 Pro and 2.5), tested on [Seedance Video](https://seedance-video.net/), a web-based Seedance AI video generator with text-to-video, image-to-video and reference-to-video.

## Write prompts like a directing brief
Seedance responds well to prompts written the way a director briefs a crew:

```
Subject: a skateboarder in a yellow jacket
Action: ollies over a curb, lands, rolls away
Camera: low tracking shot, slight slow motion
Light: late afternoon sun, long shadows
Pacing: quick start, slow finish
```

## Choosing a model version
- **2.0 Fast / 2.0 Mini**: quick drafts and storyboard checks
- **2.0 Pro / 2.5**: final renders with more detail
- **1.5 Pro**: useful baseline for comparison

## Reference images
- Up to two reference images (max 8 MB each) keep characters and products consistent.
- Use 9:16 for Shorts/Reels/TikTok and 16:9 for web and YouTube.

## Recipes
- **Product motion test**: "A glass perfume bottle rotates slowly on a reflective black surface, rim light, macro, 16:9"
- **Ad concept**: "Morning kitchen, a hand pours cereal into a bowl, top-down shot, bright natural light, 9:16"
- **Cinematic scene**: "A lighthouse at dusk, waves crash below, slow aerial pull-back, moody blue tones"
